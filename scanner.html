THE PROBLEM

Useful paper is being treated like waste.

Schools throw away worksheets, drafts, and handouts that still have blank or recoverable areas. A page printed on one side may still become notes, memos, or flashcards.

Recycling matters—but it should not always be the first step. Reuse keeps paper useful for longer and reduces the need for new paper.

THE BIG IDEA

Ask what the paper
can become.

RePaper AI combines the sheet’s condition with real school demand to recommend the most useful second life. If there is not enough usable material, it recommends recycling instead.
